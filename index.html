General Relativity vs. Quantum Mechanics

The Incompatibility of $G_{\mu\nu}$ and $\hat{H}\Psi$

General Relativity models spacetime as a continuous 4D pseudo-Riemannian manifold bent by stress-energy ($T_{\mu\nu}$). In contrast, Quantum Mechanics treats observables as non-commuting operators acting on states in a Hilbert space.

G_μν_μν + Λg_μν = (8πG / c⁴) T_μν

At quantum scales ($10^{-35}\text{m}$ Planck length), quantum zero-point fluctuations cause metric tensor $g_{\mu\nu}$ to fluctuate wildly. This creates "quantum foam", resulting in infinite curvature probabilities that cannot be renormalized using standard Quantum Field Theory (QFT).

  • Singularities: Division by zero when matter is compressed into zero volume $r \to 0$.
  • Information Paradox: Black hole evaporation (Hawking Radiation) violates quantum unitarity ($\sum P = 1$).
