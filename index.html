General Relativity vs. Quantum Mechanics

The Incompatibility of $G_{\mu\nu}$ and $\hat{H}\Psi$

General Relativity models spacetime as a continuous 4D pseudo-Riemannian manifold bent by mass-energy:

G_μν + Λg_μν = (8πG / c⁴) T_μν

At quantum scales ($10^{-35}\text{m}$ Planck length), zero-point energy fluctuations cause metric tensor $g_{\mu\nu}$ to fluctuate wildly into quantum foam, destroying the smooth manifold assumption and creating infinite, non-renormalizable probabilities.

  • Black Hole Singularities: Mathematical divide-by-zero errors occur as radius $r \to 0$.
  • Information Paradox: Hawking radiation appears thermal, violating quantum unitary probability laws ($\sum P = 1$).
